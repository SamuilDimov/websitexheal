# xHeal Email Voice Rules

Last updated: 2026-03-06
Use case: lifecycle, onboarding, newsletter, nurture, reactivation

## 1) Voice Summary
Write like a calm, clinically competent guide. Be reassuring without being soft. Be specific without sounding technical for its own sake.

## 2) Tone Settings
- Formality: moderate
- Warmth: high
- Seriousness: high
- Boldness: moderate
- Jargon: low

## 3) Core Email Formula
1. Context: what the reader is feeling or facing
2. Clarity: what changed / what matters now
3. Action: one next step

Format:
- Opening: 1-2 short paragraphs
- Body: 2-4 scannable blocks
- Close: one clear CTA

## 4) Writing Rules
Do:
- Lead with outcome and time horizon
- Use concrete phrasing (what, why, next)
- Keep sentences short and direct
- Use one primary action per email

Do not:
- Overuse hype words
- Stack multiple asks in one email
- Use fear-heavy copy
- Make medical claims beyond support/awareness

## 5) Preferred Vocabulary
- early signal
- clear next step
- care plan
- follow-up
- pattern
- timeline
- specialist-ready

Avoid vocabulary:
- revolutionary
- breakthrough cure
- game-changing
- seamless solution
- leverage/synergy

## 6) Subject Line Style
Use:
- Clear benefit + specificity
- Contrasts ("Stop X. Start Y.")
- Pattern-focused questions

Examples:
- What changed before your last flare-up?
- One clear next step for this week
- Your care timeline is ready
- Stop reacting late. Start acting early.

## 7) Preheader Style
- Extend the subject with concrete context
- 40-90 characters

Examples:
- See your likely trigger pattern and what to do today.
- Bring one specialist-ready timeline to your next visit.

## 8) CTA Standards
Primary CTA options:
- Start care now
- Get your care plan
- Build my health timeline
- View my report

Rule:
- Exactly one primary CTA button/link per email

## 9) Required Safety Line (When Needed)
Use on medical-adjacent emails:
"xHeal supports informed decisions with your care team and does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional medical care."

## 10) Reusable Blocks
Reassurance opener:
"If you’re feeling unsure about what your data means, you’re not alone. Most people track a lot and still don’t get clear answers."

Action bridge:
"Here’s the key signal we want you to look at this week, and the next step that follows from it."

Care-team bridge:
"When you share one complete timeline, appointments can focus on decisions instead of reconstructing history."

## 11) QA Checklist
- One audience segment targeted?
- One message objective?
- One CTA only?
- Evidence-based tone?
- Compliance-safe framing included if relevant?
